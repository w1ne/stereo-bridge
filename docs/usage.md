# Setup and recording

## One-time setup

Pair two **original DJI Mic Mini transmitters** in Android Bluetooth settings. A receiver is unnecessary. Open Stereo Bridge and grant Camera and Nearby devices (Android 12+). Android 10/11 use normal legacy Bluetooth access.

The app selects the two paired supported transmitters. If more than two are paired, choose the left and right devices under Advanced.

## Daily use

**Power on both paired mics, enable Bluetooth, open Stereo Bridge and tap Record. Tap Stop to save.**

Videos are stored as stereo MP4 in **Movies / Stereo Bridge**. Tap **Open last video** to watch the most recent take. Each transmitter occupies a separate audio channel. For centered voices, split the stereo track into two mono tracks in your editor.

Advanced controls provide gain, mute and channel swap. Device selections and settings survive restart and reboot. The app never starts recording automatically.

## Background capture

Recording continues when another app opens or the screen sleeps. Optional notifications show status and provide Stop. If notifications are denied, return to the app to Stop.

## If something stops

- **Permission denied:** use the app's Settings action, restore access and return to Record.
- **Mic disconnected:** check charge/power and Bluetooth, then tap Record again. A started take is finalized when possible.
- **Retry save:** free storage if necessary and tap Retry save. Recoverable video stays pending until publication succeeds.
- **Storage almost full:** free space before the next take.

Do a short playback check before an important session. [Compatibility](compatibility.md) describes the tested hardware and current limits.
